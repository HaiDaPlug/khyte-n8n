# Personal and Solo Consultant: Overview

Solo consultants and freelancers need automation but can't afford complexity. You're the entire business: sales, delivery, accounting, marketing. This section contains approximately 28 automation patterns designed for individuals managing everything themselves.

## Common Pain Points

**Pain Point 1: Everything Falls on You**
No one to delegate to. You forget follow-ups, miss opportunities, work weekends catching up.
*Automations that help*: Personal task management, automated follow-ups, time blocking.

**Pain Point 2: Networking is Inconsistent**
You network when you need work, not when you don't. Relationships decay.
*Automations that help*: Relationship reminders, touch-base automation, referral tracking.

**Pain Point 3: Learning Happens in Gaps**
Save articles but never read them. Take notes but never review them.
*Automations that help*: Reading queue management, spaced repetition, knowledge capture.

## Scope

- **Solo Workflows and Habits** (8-10 automations): Personal productivity, time management, self-accountability
- **Personal CRM and Networking** (8-12 automations): Relationship management, outreach, referrals
- **Learning and Knowledge Capture** (8-10 automations): Reading, note-taking, skill development

## Tool Landscape

- **Personal productivity**: Notion, Obsidian, Roam, Things, Todoist
- **CRM**: Airtable, Notion, Dex, HubSpot free tier
- **Reading**: Pocket, Instapaper, Matter, Readwise
- **Notes**: Notion, Obsidian, Evernote, Apple Notes
- **Scheduling**: Calendly, Cal.com, Savvycal

## Measuring Success

- **Follow-through rate**: % of commitments kept vs forgotten
- **Network health**: Frequency of meaningful touches with key contacts
- **Learning consistency**: Articles read per week, notes reviewed
- **Work-life balance**: Hours worked, weekend work frequency

## Cross-References

Solo consultant automation borrows heavily from:
- Sales (you're the sales team)
- Operations (you're the ops team)
- Marketing (you're the marketing team)

The difference: everything must be low-maintenance and work for an audience of one.
