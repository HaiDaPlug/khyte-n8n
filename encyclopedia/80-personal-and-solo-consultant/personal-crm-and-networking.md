# Personal CRM and Networking

10 automation patterns for relationship management, staying in touch, and building professional network. Focus: consistent touches without feeling fake.

---

## 1. Relationship Touch Reminder System
**Trigger**: X days since last contact (customizable per person)
**Steps**: Identify contacts you haven't touched in threshold period (30/60/90 days by tier) → send reminder with context (last conversation, shared interests) → suggest touch method (coffee, email, LinkedIn comment) → track outreach → log interaction
**Time Saved**: 1-2 hours per week | **Variants**: VIP vs casual tiers, industry event triggers

## 2. Post-Meeting Follow-Up Automation
**Trigger**: Meeting ends on calendar
**Steps**: Send thank-you email within 2 hours → include key discussion points → suggest next steps → add meeting note to contact record → schedule follow-up reminder → track if they respond
**Time Saved**: 10-15 min per meeting | **Variants**: Coffee chat vs sales meeting templates

## 3. LinkedIn Engagement Automation
**Trigger**: Daily or weekly
**Steps**: Pull posts from key contacts → like/comment on meaningful content → share relevant posts with added commentary → track engagement → identify warm contacts (recent interaction) for outreach
**Time Saved**: 30-45 min per day | **Variants**: Industry-specific, thought leadership focus

## 4. Referral Source Tracking and Appreciation
**Trigger**: New client onboarded
**Steps**: Ask "How did you hear about me?" → log referral source → send thank-you to referrer → offer reciprocal referral or gift → track referrer's contribution → prioritize relationship
**Time Saved**: 10 min per referral | **Variants**: Referral fees, partnership arrangements

## 5. Event and Conference Follow-Up
**Trigger**: After attending event
**Steps**: Import business cards or connections → send personalized follow-up ("Great meeting you at [event]") → add to CRM with event context → suggest next touch in 30 days → track conversion to opportunity
**Time Saved**: 1-2 hours per event | **Variants**: Speaker follow-ups, booth visitor tracking

## 6. Birthday and Work Anniversary Outreach
**Trigger**: Contact's birthday or anniversary
**Steps**: Send personalized message (not generic) → reference shared connection or past conversation → no sales pitch, just genuine good wishes → track responses → strengthen relationship
**Time Saved**: 5 min per person | **Variants**: Company anniversaries, milestone celebrations

## 7. Warm Introduction Request Automation
**Trigger**: Need introduction to target person/company
**Steps**: Identify mutual connections → draft introduction request → explain why you want intro and what value you offer → send to connector → track response → thank connector → follow up with new contact
**Time Saved**: 15-20 min per request | **Variants**: Reverse (offering to connect others)

## 8. Newsletter and Content Sharing
**Trigger**: Weekly or monthly
**Steps**: Curate valuable content for network → write brief commentary → segment by relevance (not everyone gets everything) → send personalized shares → track engagement → follow up with engaged recipients
**Time Saved**: 2-3 hours per send | **Variants**: Industry insights, helpful resources

## 9. Networking Goal Tracking
**Trigger**: Monthly review
**Steps**: Set networking goals (X new connections, Y coffee chats, Z introductions made) → track progress → identify gaps → suggest actions to reach goals → celebrate achievements
**Time Saved**: Accountability system | **Variants**: Network diversity goals, industry-specific targets

## 10. Cold Outreach Personalization at Scale
**Trigger**: Prospecting campaign
**Steps**: Research prospect (company, role, recent activity) → identify personalization hooks → draft customized message → reference specific context → send → track response → follow up appropriately
**Time Saved**: 10-15 min per prospect | **Variants**: Multi-touch sequences, A/B testing

---
