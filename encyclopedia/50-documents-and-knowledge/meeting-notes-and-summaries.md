# Meeting Notes and Summaries

9 automation patterns for meeting documentation, action tracking, and knowledge capture. Focus: saving meeting time and ensuring follow-through.

---

## 1. Auto-Transcription and Summary
**Trigger**: Meeting starts/ends
**Steps**: Record and transcribe meeting → identify speakers → use AI to generate summary (3-5 key points) → extract decisions made → list topics discussed → format as document → send to attendees within 30 min
**Time Saved**: 20-30 min per meeting | **Variants**: Real-time vs post-meeting, speaker identification

## 2. Action Item Extraction and Assignment
**Trigger**: Meeting ends
**Steps**: Analyze transcript for action items ("John will...", "We need to...") → extract task, owner, deadline → create tasks in PM tool → assign to owners → send summary of actions to all attendees → track completion
**Time Saved**: 10-15 min per meeting | **Variants**: Auto-deadlines based on urgency keywords

## 3. Meeting Decision Log
**Trigger**: Decision made in meeting
**Steps**: Detect decision language in transcript → extract decision and rationale → add to decision log with context → tag with project/topic → link to related decisions → make searchable for future reference
**Time Saved**: Organizational memory (invaluable) | **Variants**: Vote tracking, reversal detection

## 4. Recurring Meeting Agenda Pre-Population
**Trigger**: 24 hours before recurring meeting
**Steps**: Create agenda from template → pull action item status from last meeting → add "Review action items" section → collect new agenda items from team → pull relevant metrics/data → send to attendees with pre-read
**Time Saved**: 15-20 min per meeting | **Variants**: Team inputs, dynamic sections

## 5. Meeting Minutes Distribution
**Trigger**: Meeting ends + notes reviewed
**Steps**: Format notes into standard minutes template → include attendees, topics, decisions, actions → send to attendees and stakeholders → file in project/client folder → update project timeline based on decisions
**Time Saved**: 15 min per meeting | **Variants**: Client-ready formatting, multi-language

## 6. Key Quote and Insight Extraction
**Trigger**: Meeting transcription complete
**Steps**: Use AI to identify notable quotes, insights, or concerns → highlight for review → categorize (customer feedback, product ideas, risks, wins) → route to appropriate teams → add to insight repository
**Time Saved**: Captures what would be forgotten | **Variants**: Customer meeting specific

## 7. Meeting Follow-Up Reminder Automation
**Trigger**: 3 days after meeting
**Steps**: Check if action items from meeting completed → send reminder to owners of incomplete items → include context (what meeting, what was decided) → escalate if repeatedly ignored → notify meeting organizer of status
**Time Saved**: 10 min per meeting follow-up | **Variants**: Custom timing, chain of responsibility

## 8. Client Meeting Brief Auto-Generation
**Trigger**: Client meeting scheduled
**Steps**: Pull client history (past meetings, open deals, support tickets, recent communications) → summarize recent activity → identify topics likely to come up → suggest talking points → send brief to meeting attendees 24 hours before
**Time Saved**: 20-30 min per meeting | **Variants**: Internal meetings, board meeting prep

## 9. Meeting Effectiveness Metrics
**Trigger**: Weekly/monthly rollup
**Steps**: Track meetings by type, duration, attendee count → calculate cost (time × avg salary) → measure outcomes (decisions, actions) → identify low-value meetings (no actions, few attendees engaged) → suggest cancellations or format changes
**Time Saved**: Reduces meeting waste | **Variants**: Department-specific analysis

---

2. **Action Item Extraction** (#2) - Immediate follow-through improvement
3. **Recurring Meeting Agenda** (#4) - Makes meetings more productive
4. **Client Meeting Brief** (#8) - Customer-facing professionalism
