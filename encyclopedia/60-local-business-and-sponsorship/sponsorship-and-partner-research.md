# Sponsorship and Partner Research

9 automation patterns for finding sponsors, tracking relationships, and managing partnership outreach. Focus: systematic prospecting.

---

## 1. Local Business Sponsor Prospecting
**Trigger**: Weekly or on-demand
**Steps**: Define sponsor criteria (industry, location, size) → search business directories, LinkedIn, local chambers → enrich with contact data → score by fit → add to sponsor CRM → tag with approach angle → create outreach sequence
**Time Saved**: 3-4 hours/week vs manual research | **Variants**: Event-specific, tiered sponsorship levels

## 2. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Matching
**Trigger**: New sponsor target identified
**Steps**: Research company's CSR focus and values → match to your cause/event mission → identify alignment opportunities → personalize pitch angle → generate custom proposal draft → track CSR budget cycles
**Time Saved**: 30-45 min per prospect | **Variants**: Environmental, education, community focus areas

## 3. Sponsor Renewal Tracking and Outreach
**Trigger**: 90/60/30 days before sponsor contract ends
**Steps**: Identify expiring sponsors → pull performance data from last year → calculate sponsor ROI (impressions, engagement, leads) → generate renewal proposal with results → schedule renewal call → track renewal decision
**Time Saved**: 1-2 hours per sponsor | **Variants**: Early bird discounts, multi-year offers

## 4. Partnership Opportunity Alerts
**Trigger**: New local event, business opening, or community initiative announced
**Steps**: Monitor local news, business journals, permit filings → detect partnership opportunities → enrich with contact info → send alert to partnership team with context → suggest approach
**Time Saved**: 5-10 hours/week on manual monitoring | **Variants**: Industry-specific monitoring

## 5. Sponsor Contact Database Maintenance
**Trigger**: Quarterly review or contact change detected
**Steps**: Verify sponsor contacts still at company → update job titles → check LinkedIn for changes → enrich with new contacts if original left → flag stale contacts → track decision-maker changes
**Time Saved**: 2-3 hours/quarter | **Variants**: LinkedIn integration, auto-enrichment

## 6. Sponsorship Proposal Auto-Generation
**Trigger**: Qualified sponsor identified
**Steps**: Pull sponsor details and preferences → select appropriate package tier → customize proposal template → add case studies from similar sponsors → calculate projected ROI → generate branded PDF → send for review
**Time Saved**: 45-60 min per proposal | **Variants**: A/B tested templates, video proposals

## 7. Sponsor Activation Tracking
**Trigger**: Sponsor commitment signed
**Steps**: Create sponsor activation checklist → track deliverables (logo placement, social mentions, booth setup, speaking slot) → assign tasks to team → send status updates to sponsor → flag missed deliverables → measure sponsor exposure
**Time Saved**: 30-45 min per sponsor | **Variants**: Event-specific vs ongoing sponsorships

## 8. Community Event Calendar Monitoring
**Trigger**: Daily scan of community calendars
**Steps**: Monitor local event listings, festival schedules, fair dates → identify events relevant to your audience → reach out to event organizers → explore sponsorship or partnership → add to outreach calendar
**Time Saved**: 2-3 hours/week | **Variants**: Geographic filters, industry filters

## 9. Sponsor Impact Reporting Automation
**Trigger**: After sponsored event or quarterly
**Steps**: Compile sponsor metrics (impressions, mentions, attendee interactions, leads generated) → compare to sponsor goals → generate impact report with visuals → use AI to write summary → send to sponsor with thank you → identify upsell opportunities
**Time Saved**: 1-2 hours per report | **Variants**: Real-time dashboards, social media analytics
