# Customer Communication

9 automation patterns for local business customer engagement, loyalty, and reputation management. Focus: personal touch at scale.

---

## 1. Birthday and Anniversary Messaging
**Trigger**: Customer birthday or anniversary of first purchase
**Steps**: Identify customers with birthday/anniversary this week → send personalized message (SMS or email) → include special offer or discount → track redemptions → build customer loyalty
**Time Saved**: 1-2 hours/week | **Variants**: VIP customer special treatment, tiered offers

## 2. Post-Visit Review Request Automation
**Trigger**: 2-3 days after customer visit/purchase
**Steps**: Send review request via email or SMS → include direct links to Google, Yelp, Facebook → personalize with service details → track response rate → thank customers who leave reviews → flag negative reviews for immediate response
**Time Saved**: 30-60 min/day | **Variants**: Incentivized reviews (within guidelines), multi-platform requests

## 3. Loyalty Program Points and Rewards Tracking
**Trigger**: Customer purchase or visit
**Steps**: Update loyalty points balance → check if reward tier reached → send notification of new points → alert when reward unlocked → send reminder of expiring points → track redemption rates
**Time Saved**: Automated vs manual punch cards | **Variants**: Tiered programs, referral bonuses

## 4. Inactive Customer Re-Engagement
**Trigger**: Customer hasn't visited in 60+ days
**Steps**: Segment by last visit date → create win-back offer → send personalized "We miss you" message → track response and return visits → remove non-responders from active list after 2-3 attempts
**Time Saved**: 2-3 hours/month | **Variants**: Segment by previous spend level, seasonal businesses

## 5. Local Event and Promotion Announcements
**Trigger**: New event, sale, or special offering scheduled
**Steps**: Segment customers by preferences and location → personalize announcement → schedule send for optimal day/time → include booking or RSVP link → track engagement and conversions
**Time Saved**: 1-2 hours per announcement | **Variants**: VIP early access, location-based targeting

## 6. Customer Feedback Collection and Analysis
**Trigger**: After service delivery or monthly survey schedule
**Steps**: Send feedback request → collect ratings and comments → use AI to categorize feedback themes → flag urgent issues → route to appropriate team → track response and resolution → aggregate insights for improvement
**Time Saved**: 2-3 hours/week on manual aggregation | **Variants**: NPS tracking, service-specific surveys

## 7. Automated Thank You Messages
**Trigger**: Customer purchase, booking, or positive review
**Steps**: Send immediate thank you message → personalize with purchase/service details → include referral request for VIP customers → suggest related products/services → track customer sentiment
**Time Saved**: 15-30 min/day | **Variants**: Video thank yous for high-value customers

## 8. Social Media Shoutout Automation
**Trigger**: Customer tags business in social post
**Steps**: Detect tag or mention → review post content → if positive, like/comment/share → request permission to repost → add to UGC library → thank customer publicly → track engagement
**Time Saved**: 30-60 min/day on social monitoring | **Variants**: Contest entries, customer stories

## 9. Neighborhood and Community Updates
**Trigger**: Weekly or monthly
**Steps**: Compile community news, local partnerships, business updates → create neighborhood newsletter → personalize by customer location → include local event calendar → send to subscribers → track open/click rates
**Time Saved**: 2-3 hours per newsletter | **Variants**: Seasonal focus, hyper-local targeting
