# Finance and Back Office: Overview

Finance and accounting teams deal with high-stakes, low-margin-for-error work. A missed invoice costs revenue. A late payment damages vendor relationships. Bad bookkeeping creates tax nightmares. This section contains approximately 16 automation patterns focused on invoicing, cash flow monitoring, and bookkeeping support.

## Common Pain Points

**Pain Point 1: Manual Invoice Creation**
Copy-pasting from contracts into invoice templates, sending PDFs, tracking payments manually.
*Automations that help*: Automated invoice generation, payment tracking, overdue reminders.

**Pain Point 2: Cash Flow Surprises**
Money goes out faster than it comes in, but you don't realize until it's a problem.
*Automations that help*: Daily cash position reports, payment forecasting, low-balance alerts.

**Pain Point 3: Receipts and Expenses Everywhere**
Email inboxes, photo rolls, desk drawers—expense documentation is chaos.
*Automations that help*: Receipt capture and categorization, expense auto-categorization, bookkeeping prep.

## Scope

- **Invoices and Receipts** (8-10 automations): Creation, delivery, tracking, reconciliation
- **Cash Flow and Summaries** (5-8 automations): Monitoring, forecasting, alerts
- **Bookkeeping Support** (3-6 automations): Transaction categorization, reconciliation assistance, tax prep

## What Makes Finance Automation Different

Finance automation must be accurate, auditable, and conservative. A marketing automation error annoys one prospect. A finance automation error could mean sending the wrong invoice amount, missing tax deadlines, or regulatory violations.

Best practices:
- **Human review on critical paths**: Auto-generate invoices, but review before sending
- **Audit trails**: Log every change, every decision
- **Conservative defaults**: When in doubt, flag for human review
- **Reconciliation checks**: Ensure automation matches reality (bank vs CRM vs accounting system)

## Tool Landscape

Finance teams work with:
- **Accounting**: QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, Wave
- **Invoicing**: Stripe, Square, Bill.com, or accounting system
- **Banking**: Bank APIs, Plaid for aggregation
- **Expense management**: Expensify, Divvy, Ramp

Automation platforms connect these into workflows: invoice → payment → bookkeeping → reporting.

## Measuring Success

- **Time savings**: Hours spent on invoicing, expense processing, reconciliation
- **Cash flow improvement**: Days sales outstanding (DSO), on-time invoice delivery
- **Accuracy**: Reconciliation errors, categorization accuracy
- **Compliance**: Tax filing timeliness, audit readiness

## Cross-References

Finance connects to:
- Sales (deals → invoices)
- Operations (expenses, payroll)
- Every department (budget tracking, spend approval)
