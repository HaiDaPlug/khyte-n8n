# Creative and Content Teams: Overview

Creative teams (designers, writers, video producers, content creators) need different automation than operations teams. The work is inherently creative and non-linear, but the processes around it—idea capture, asset management, publishing—can and should be automated. This section contains approximately 26 automation patterns for creative workflow support.

## Common Pain Points

**Pain Point 1: Ideas Get Lost**
Brilliant concept mentioned in Slack, forgotten by next day. No systematic capture.
*Automations that help*: Idea capture workflows, inspiration libraries, searchable repositories.

**Pain Point 2: Asset Chaos**
Can't find the right file, multiple versions everywhere, no metadata, broken links.
*Automations that help*: Auto-tagging, version control, asset libraries, metadata extraction.

**Pain Point 3: Publishing is Manual and Slow**
Export, format, upload, schedule, cross-post—hours of work after creative is done.
*Automations that help*: Publishing pipelines, format conversion, multi-platform distribution.

## Scope

- **Idea Generation and Organizing** (8-12 automations): Capture, organize, develop ideas
- **Asset Management and Metadata** (8-10 automations): Organization, tagging, search, version control
- **Publishing Pipelines** (8-10 automations): Format conversion, distribution, scheduling, tracking

## Tool Landscape

- **Creative tools**: Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Canva
- **Asset management**: DAM systems, Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io
- **Publishing**: WordPress, Medium, YouTube, social media platforms
- **Collaboration**: Notion, Miro, FigJam, Slack

## Measuring Success

- **Time savings**: Hours spent on asset management, publishing logistics
- **Idea conversion**: % of captured ideas that become published content
- **Asset findability**: Time to find specific asset (before vs after)
- **Publishing consistency**: On-time publication rate

## Cross-References

Creative work feeds marketing (content for campaigns), sales (collateral), and knowledge management (documentation).
