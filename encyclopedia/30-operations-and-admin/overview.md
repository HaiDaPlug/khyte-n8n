# Operations and Admin: Overview

## Scope of This Section

Operations and administrative teams keep the business running but often drown in repetitive tasks: onboarding new hires, sending reminders for recurring tasks, routing approval requests, managing handoffs between departments. This section contains approximately 26 automation patterns designed to reduce administrative burden and improve operational consistency.

The automations here focus on:
- **Onboarding and offboarding**: Making employee lifecycle transitions smooth and complete
- **Recurring tasks and reminders**: Ensuring nothing falls through the cracks on routine schedules
- **Approvals and handovers**: Moving work between people and teams efficiently

These patterns are valuable for any business with 5+ people, where coordination overhead starts to hurt productivity.

## Common Pain Points Addressed

**Pain Point 1: Onboarding Takes Forever**
Manual checklist tracking, forgotten steps, inconsistent experience. New hires wait days for access.
*Automations that help*: Onboarding workflows, automated provisioning, day-based task sequences.

**Pain Point 2: Things Only Get Done When Someone Remembers**
Monthly reports, quarterly reviews, annual renewals—all forgotten until crisis.
*Automations that help*: Scheduled reminders, recurring task creation, deadline tracking.

**Pain Point 3: Approval Bottlenecks**
Requests sit in someone's inbox for days. No one knows where requests are stuck.
*Automations that help*: Approval routing, escalation workflows, status visibility.

**Pain Point 4: Handoffs Get Dropped**
Sales to operations, project to delivery, support to engineering—handoffs are where work dies.
*Automations that help*: Handoff checklists, notification systems, accountability tracking.

## What Makes Operations Automation Different

Operations automation must be reliable and visible. When sales automation fails, you lose one opportunity. When operations automation fails, the business might not notice for days—until someone misses payroll or a new hire shows up with no computer.

Best practices:
- **Redundancy**: Alert multiple people for critical tasks
- **Audit trails**: Log every step for accountability
- **Graceful degradation**: If automation fails, it should create a manual task, not silently disappear
- **Human checkpoints**: Critical operations should have human verification

## Tool Landscape

Operations teams typically work with:
- **Project management**: ClickUp, Asana, Monday, Trello
- **HR systems**: BambooHR, Gusto, Workday
- **Communication**: Slack, Teams, Email
- **Documentation**: Notion, Confluence, Google Docs
- **Forms and workflows**: Typeform, JotForm, Google Forms

Automation platforms like n8n, Zapier, and Make connect these tools into coherent workflows.

## Implementation Strategy

Week 1: Start with recurring reminders (low risk, immediate value)
Week 2: Add onboarding workflows (high impact, testable with next hire)
Week 3: Implement approval routing (solve visible bottleneck)
Week 4: Build handoff automations (cross-team coordination)

## Measuring Success

Track:
- **Time savings**: Hours spent on admin tasks (before vs after)
- **Consistency**: Onboarding completion rate, recurring task completion rate
- **Speed**: Time from request to approval, handoff completion time
- **Error reduction**: Missed tasks, forgotten steps, lost requests
